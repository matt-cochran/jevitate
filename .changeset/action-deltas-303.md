Opt-in `--action-deltas` (MCP `actionDeltas`, check-suite item option `actionDeltas`; `explore --strategy goal` and `usability`) records what each action changed on the page (#303). Off by default: no capture, no prompt or result changes, and no-progress works exactly as before. On: an accessibility snapshot before and after the action, the toasts and banners announced in between, the action's requests (method, path, status) and the URL and title, redacted first (registered secrets, password and secret-marked field values, credential-shaped values). Self-changing nodes (clocks, carousels, counters) are learned per route from no-action snapshots and dropped; the rest is ranked by closeness to the action and big changes are summarised. Code decides a verdict per action: `no-change` (nothing changed and no request was sent — the only verdict that counts toward a no-progress stop), `relevant-change`, or `inconclusive` (never progress or no-progress). Jev may label untied changes and propose per-route ignore rules, accepted only for nodes code saw change on their own. Each delta is attached to its transcript step (also in the `--json` result) and Recording step (`delta`), the result gains `actionDeltas` (verdict counts, overhead), the human output a `DELTA` line per recent step, and one bounded line goes into the model's step history; the expected change (navigation, the typed value, the sent message, a toggled state) is checked against it. Overhead: about 50–110 ms per action on a small page, 0.35–0.5 s on a 400-row page. The flag also applies to `journey run`, `journey annotate`, `journey demo`, `verify-fix` (MCP `run_journey`, `annotate_journey`, `demo_journey`, `verify_fix` take `actionDeltas`) and to adversarial and coverage runs: replays compare each step's delta with the one its Recording stored (a mismatch is evidence, never a verdict), annotate drafts expected results from deltas, demo captions steps with them, adversarial defects carry the delta of their action, and coverage lists actions with no effect. A goal-run write that changed the page is re-checked once after a reload (a GET, never a re-post): `persisted: yes | no | inconclusive`. A report may quote text an action announced (a toast gone before the report).
