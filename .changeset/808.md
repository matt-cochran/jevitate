A stale approval with fresh evidence is a human handoff, not a loop (#808). `journey readiness` (and `journey bundle`) list a strict-review item only a person's approval settles, such as "Changed since approval" or "Catalog links need re-review", under `awaitingHuman` with the owner's hash-bound commands, and no longer send you back to `journey review --strict`. Strict review and CI still fail until the owner approves; nothing is approved or waived. The `jevitate-promotion-ready` skill names this case.
