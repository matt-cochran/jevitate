Strict review no longer blames an unchanged Journey for a proof from another jevitate version (#816). A stale proof now carries `staleCause`: `journey-changed`, `engine` (the Journey is unchanged, but the engine that recorded the proof computes its identity differently), `read-policy` (the target's `safety.readRequests` changed) `engine-unknown` (the receipt names no engine: written before 0.30.0) or `no-planner-identity`. Every verify receipt now stores `engine: {version, commit}`, so `engine` is read from the receipt, not inferred, and under `require-sensitive` a receipt that names no engine never counts. The review line, the policy reason and the readiness detail name the cause, and for `engine` recommend reviewing with the version that recorded the proof, or re-running `journey verify --mutate`. A mismatched proof still fails a policy that requires one.
