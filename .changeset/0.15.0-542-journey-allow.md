`journey run` and `journey verify` take `--allow <origin>` (repeatable), so `verify --mutate` can block a write to another origin and a split-origin app's Journey reaches a conclusive mutation proof (#542). `explore-author-journey` records the origins it was given as `metadata.allowedOrigins`. A replay's allowlist is the recorded site (or the `--env` origins), plus `metadata.allowedOrigins`, plus `--allow`. MCP `run_journey` and `verify` take no `allow` argument: they get the recorded origins.
