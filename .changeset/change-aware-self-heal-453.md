Change-aware, budgeted self-heal that proposes a reviewed Journey revision (#453). `journey run <id> --self-heal hybrid|full` now requires change context: `--changes <git-range>` (read-only; only resolved commit SHAs reach git) and/or `--change-note "<text>"`. A broken step is healed only when the change explains it (its anchor was renamed in the range or a note), and only by retargeting that step: assertions, success checks, invariants and write/irreversible steps are never rewritten, and a click or fill is healed only when its recorded requests are reads, it matches no risky control, and any mutating request during the probe is blocked. The healer proposes candidates from the observed controls and the change facts; it never acts on the page, and raw diffs never reach a model. Each broken step and the run have a heal budget (`--heal-max-attempts`, `--heal-max-model-calls`, `--heal-max-ms`, `--heal-max-run-attempts`, `--heal-max-run-ms`). A successful heal reports `healed-pending-review` (new exit code 5) and writes a proposed revision to `.proposals/<journeyId>.json` with per-step justification and before/after evidence; the stored Journey is unchanged until `jevitate journey promote <id> --proposal <pid>` accepts it through the normal approval path (`--reject-proposal <pid> --reason` rejects it), and `journey review` shows it. A heal that runs out of budget ends `heal-exhausted` (exit 1) with a full attempt log (hypothesis, change evidence, candidate, observation, rejection reason) in result.json and `jevitate report`; an unexplained break is reported as a likely regression. `jevitate check --self-heal --changes <base>..HEAD` re-runs a failed Journey once with the change context; a pending revision makes the check exit 5 (JUnit failure type `healed-pending-review`, SARIF warning). The legacy `healed` outcome (exit 0) is gone; the default remains fail-closed.
