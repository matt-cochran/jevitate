`journey run --allow <origin>` and `journey verify --allow <origin>` now let a step navigate to that origin under `--env` or `--base-url` (#803). Before, the origin check that runs before any browser read only the environment's own `allow` list. It refused the step with `E_ENV_ORIGIN_REFUSED` (exit 64), even though the help says `--allow` adds the origin. The refusal message now lists the `--allow` origins too, and it names `--allow` as a fix.
